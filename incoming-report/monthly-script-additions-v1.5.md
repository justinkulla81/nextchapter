# Monthly report: additions from version 1.5

These items are added to the existing monthly steps. Keep the same rules:

- Reference period is the 1st to the last day of the month.
- Nothing dated after it.
- No NextChapter customer data.
- Label sources as "What we measure", "What others find" and "What this may mean".

## Monthly (when a new release is out)

**Career ladders (Chapter 09)**

- Check for new releases from Workday, Gusto, ADP Research, Revelio Labs and Burning Glass Institute.
- Update the career-ladder table, and only with figures dated inside the reference period.
- Label all of it as private-platform data.

**Employer training (Chapter 09)**

- Training magazine's Training Industry Report usually comes out in November.
- ATD's State of the Industry usually comes out in May.
- SHRM's benefits survey usually comes out in June.
- Keep the note that Training magazine and ATD disagree until both point the same way.

**Colleges and AI (Chapter 09)**

Add any new AI degrees, requirements or prices announced in the month. Watch for:

- the ABET AI/ML criteria final vote (Nov 6, 2026)
- AAMC's final AI competencies
- GMAC's Application Trends (October)
- National Student Clearinghouse enrollment releases
- any placement or wage data for AI programs (this is the key gap)

**Public retraining (Chapter 13)**

- The stopgap funding deadline is Dec 11, 2026, and FY2027 appropriations are still open (the House bill cuts WIOA Title I by 62%).
- Track the H.R. 8210 floor vote and any Rapid Reskill grant (TEGL 15-25) awards.
- Use the Opportunity Data tracker for Workforce Pell approval counts.
- Watch for the DOL AI Workforce Hub launch and new TEGLs.
- WIOA performance data come out once a year (around November).

## Quarterly (Q3 addendum)

- **Industry productivity versus long searches** is an annual feature for each Q3 edition. Run `code/08_industry_productivity.py` and update the BLS total factor productivity values for major industries; the 2025 data are expected around December 2026.
- **State comparison:** add a state comparison using BLS state productivity (released each May) against long-term unemployment by state.

## Research agenda only (no numbers until the thresholds are met)

- Productivity per AI token.
- Productivity by function, seniority and place.
