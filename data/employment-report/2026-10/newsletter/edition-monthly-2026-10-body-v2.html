Our October White Collar Employment Report is out. The short version:

  • Hiring picked up. Employers added 165,000 jobs in October and unemployment edged down to 4.1% (BLS, seasonally adjusted).
  • But searches are longer. 28.5% of unemployed white-collar workers have been out six months or more, up from 23.2% a year ago. At ages 55 to 64 it is 39.1%, up from 27.0%, about 4 in 10. Most of them lost a job, so it is not mainly retirement (Census CPS microdata, January to September, our calculation).
  • AI skills and pay. Jobs that name AI skills post a median 22% more than similar jobs across employers, but about 1% more at the same employer. The observed premium is mainly an employer difference (our own snapshot of open jobs).
  • Where jobs moved. Health care added 519,000 jobs over the year. Information lost 119,000, about three-quarters of it in telecommunications, data hosting and film rather than software publishers (BLS).
