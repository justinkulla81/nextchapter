# State and local programs: Texas, Oklahoma, Louisiana, Arkansas

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

- Each program's own official page was opened with WebFetch during this session. Verified? = Yes only when the amount or eligibility in the row appears on that page. Rows built from search snippets, local-board homepages that gave no detail, or pages that returned 403/404 are Verified? = No.
- The WebSearch quota ran out partway through (Oklahoma and Louisiana local boards were hit hardest), so those states have fewer local rows than the brief asks for. Pages that blocked or failed are listed in each Gaps line.
- Most local workforce boards publish no dollar caps for training scholarships. Where a page said "amount not stated" that is what the row says.
- Federal baseline programs are left out unless the state or board runs them with state-specific rules.
- Counts below were computed from the tables.

Counts: see the line under each state heading.
