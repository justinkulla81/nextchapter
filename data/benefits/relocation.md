# Relocation / "Get Paid to Move" Incentive Catalog (as of 2026-10-22)

## Read this first (method + limits)
- WebSearch budget was exhausted early (200/turn cap shared). Almost all findings come from WebFetch of (a) official program pages that loaded, and (b) MakeMyMove (MMM) pages, used as a LEAD list only.
- Many official sites were blocked (HTTP 403: hrsa.gov, michigan.gov, accd.vermont.gov, amplifybloomington.com) or timed out (studentaid.gov). Those rows are marked accordingly.
- **Verified column key**
  - **Y** = official page fetched and read in this session.
  - **S** = secondary: the MMM program page was fetched (MMM is an aggregator/operator; it often keeps stale listings). Official city/chamber site NOT checked.
  - **L** = listing-only: just the MMM index card (value and "cash included" flag). Details and status NOT checked. MMM index "value" includes non-cash perks, so it is not a cash amount.
  - **U** = unverified: from a search snippet, news item, job posting, or my background knowledge; the source page was not read.
- MMM index (https://www.makemymove.com/get-paid/more, pages 1-17, 293 programs when fetched) does not show open/closed status, so L rows carry "status unknown".
- Proven stale-listing example: MMM lists Choose Macon ($7,325), but the official page says that program has concluded. Treat every S/L "open" claim as a lead, not a fact.
- Not researched or nothing found (budget exhausted): CO, ID, MA, MT, NH, ND, RI, SD, UT, WY, and any non-MMM city programs in VA. Absence here does not mean no program exists.
