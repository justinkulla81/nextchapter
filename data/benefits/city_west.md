# City, county, town and regional money for professionals: Western states

Collected 2026-10-22. Method note: WebFetch safety checks were rate-limited and many city sites returned 404 or blocked automated fetches, so most rows are leads (Verified = No). 'Verified = Yes' means the program's own page confirmed it. No official city/county remote-worker or newcomer programs were found and confirmed for NM, AZ, UT, WY, MT, ID, NV, OR, AK, HI in this pass; MakeMyMove, Forbes and news roundups surfaced none in those states and are not treated as proof.
