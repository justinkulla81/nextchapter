# Free / sponsored learning catalog for career changers (as of 2026-10-22)

**Verification key (last column):** V = official page fetched this session; S = confirmed only via web-search snippets (third-party/mixed); K = from prior knowledge, NOT re-checked this session. Treat K rows as leads: confirm price, status and eligibility on the URL before telling a user. Status "Open" means no closure was seen, not that it was re-confirmed. Costs: "Free" = no cost for learning; "Exam $" = optional paid credential.

Key findings from live checks:
- Google AI Essentials on Coursera now lists $49/mo with a 7-day trial (V). The free route is sponsor-funded (Goodwill, workforce boards, libraries, states, Coursera partners). Check each.
- OpenAI Academy shows four tracks (Apply AI at Work, Build with AI, Lead AI Adoption, Teach and learn with AI); the page names no certifications and no price (V). A formal paid "OpenAI Certifications" pilot is reported by third parties only (S).
- Anthropic Academy (Skilljar) courses are free, with completion certificates; a ~$99 proctored exam is reported by blogs only, unconfirmed (S).
- AWS AI & ML Scholars 2026 applications are closed (S). AWS Student Rewards (students only) launched Aug 2026 (S).
- Microsoft Elevate / AI Skills Navigator: free workforce-board LinkedIn Learning AI courses (Mar 2026, S). I could not confirm general free LinkedIn Learning access.
- IBM SkillsBuild: "100% free", includes adult learners (V).
- Not confirmed anywhere: Google "AI Works", any Coursera "Career Gains" free tier, CompTIA free vouchers for unemployed (CompTIA main page lists none, V).
