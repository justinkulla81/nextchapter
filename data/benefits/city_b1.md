# City / county / regional money for newcomers and residents (batch 1)
Checked 2026-10-22. Columns: Name | State | Locality | What it pays | Eligibility | Status | Official URL | Verified.
Method notes: DuckDuckGo HTML showed a CAPTCHA (not bypassed) and Brave Search put up a bot wall, so discovery used Bing result lists plus search snippets; aggregators were leads only. Several official city sites (Charlotte, Raleigh, Durham, Arlington VA, Greensboro) returned 403/Cloudflare to curl, so those rows are Verified = No. "Verified = Yes" only where the official page itself showed program, amount and that it is currently open. Rows listed as Closed were read on the official page but are not "open", so Verified = No.
Remote-worker "pay to move" programs: no currently open city/county program was found in any of the six states. The one found (Ruston, LA) is closed. Aggregator lists reviewed (Fairway, MoveBuddha, MakeMyMove article, MovingRated) named none in these six states.

## New Hampshire
none found. No city/town/county newcomer, remote-worker, homebuyer or displaced-worker program located that was not state-run (NH Housing and NH Works are state-level and excluded). Not exhaustive.
