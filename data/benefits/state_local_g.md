# State and local retraining programs, part G (NC, VA, MD, DC, WV, TN)

Research date: 2026-10-23.

Method and limits: Pages were opened with WebFetch this session. Verified? = Yes only when the amount or eligibility written in the row appeared on the official page that was fetched. Rows marked No come from search snippets, news, statutes, unreadable PDFs, or pages that returned 403/404; treat them as leads. WebSearch was available but returned mostly secondary sources. Many official sites (THEC, tn.gov, SCHEV, DOE Virginia, nccommunitycolleges.edu, several Maryland and DC pages) returned 403/404 or unreadable PDFs. Per-person ITA dollar caps were not stated on any page that could be read, in any of the six jurisdictions. Self-Employment Assistance, UI approved-training rules and Shared Work were checked for each jurisdiction; findings are in each Gaps line.

Counts (rows / verified Yes): North Carolina 14 / 7. Virginia 13 / 9. Maryland 14 / 10. District of Columbia 10 / 6. West Virginia 9 / 6. Tennessee 9 / 4.
