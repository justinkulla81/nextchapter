# Publicly funded adult retraining / upskilling programs, Part 1 (AL, AK, AZ, AR, CA, CO, CT, DE, DC, FL, GA)

Research date: 2026-10-22.

Method and limits, read first:
- The web-search quota ran out after 5 searches (AL x2, AK, AZ, and a failed AR search). Everything else came from direct fetches of official pages (WebFetch and curl).
- Several official sites blocked automated fetches (403/404/DNS): azcommerce.com, des.az.gov, ark.org/adhe, sfa.adhe.edu, cdle.colorado.gov, and some cwdc.colorado.gov and cdhe.colorado.gov deep links.
- "Verified = yes" means I opened the official page and the cited facts appear on it. "Verified = no" means the entry comes from a search snippet or from my background knowledge. Treat the amount and eligibility cells on "no" rows as unconfirmed leads, and confirm each one at the URL before use.
- "Status" is the status shown on the page. Where the page gave none, it says "not stated".
- Federal programs (WIOA, TAA, Pell) are included only where the state page presents them as the state's own offering.
- Gaps: no state-provided Coursera, LinkedIn Learning or Google certificate access was verified for any of these states. No state AI-reskilling program was verified except the California item, which is unverified. No state self-employment assistance (SEA) program was verified. No tech-bootcamp subsidy was verified.
