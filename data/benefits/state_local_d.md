# State and local programs, sweep D (FL, GA, AL, MS, SC)

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

Pages were read with direct page fetches (WebFetch, and curl for pages the fetch tool blocked) on official .gov, .edu, workforce-board and state-legislature sites. Verified? = Yes only when the page was opened this session and the facts in the row appear on it. The web-search budget ran out partway through, so Alabama, Mississippi and South Carolina local boards were reached by guessing official URLs and following site links, not by search. Several official sites blocked automated reads (CareerSource Tampa Bay and Florida Complete returned 403 or a bot check; Alabama and Mississippi workforce-board sites are partly script-rendered). Dollar caps for WIOA Individual Training Accounts are set by each local board and were not published on any page I could open, so rows say "not stated on page" rather than guess. Rows marked Verified? = No say why in Eligibility.

Counts (rows / verified Yes): Florida 19 / 15, Georgia 12 / 9, Alabama 12 / 12, Mississippi 10 / 8, South Carolina 14 / 14.
