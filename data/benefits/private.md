# Private, Nonprofit and Philanthropic Money for Laid-Off Professionals and Working Families (as of 2026-10-22)

**Verification key.** `Y` = confirmed by 2026 search results/news this session (official page itself NOT always fetched). `P` = partly confirmed (existence yes; terms/status stale or conflicting). `B` = background knowledge only, URL not re-checked this session; confirm on the page before telling any user. URLs are best-known official addresses; where not confirmed they are marked "(unconfirmed)". Nothing here was confirmed via a logged-in or applied-to flow.

**Global caveats.** (1) Almost no private fund pays for "lost work alone"; most require a documented emergency (rent, utilities, medical) and a membership/profession tie. (2) Many AI-displacement funds announced in 2026 are research/pilot money, not a direct-apply benefit. (3) Guaranteed-income pilots are lottery-based, geographically bounded, and mostly closed to new applicants. (4) Beware fee-charging "grant" scams.
