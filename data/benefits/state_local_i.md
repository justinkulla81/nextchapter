# State and local programs for laid-off and career-changing professionals: Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont, Maine

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

- Sweep for Massachusetts, New Hampshire, Vermont and Maine. Every row is something a person can use: tuition, a training seat, UI-linked help, coaching or a free credential.
- Verified? = Yes only where the official page was opened this session and the figures in the row appear on it. Direct fetches of mass.gov and nhes.nh.gov returned HTTP 403, so the mass.gov rows marked Yes (TOP, ITA, WorkShare, Rapid Response, Google AI release) were read from an archived copy of the official mass.gov page, and each is cross-checked against a live MassHire board or career center page where one exists. Treat those as Yes with that caveat.
- The web search budget ran out partway through, so New Hampshire has the thinnest coverage and no NHES pages could be read.
- Employer-only grants (Workforce Training Fund, FAST AI, WorkInvestNH, Workforce Expansion Program) are not rows; see each state's Left out note.
- Counts: Massachusetts 30 rows (30 verified), New Hampshire 11 rows (9 verified), Vermont 18 rows (17 verified), Maine 16 rows (14 verified).
