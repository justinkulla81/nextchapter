# State and local programs, group E: Kentucky, Pennsylvania, Ohio, Michigan, Indiana

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

- Source: official pages opened with WebFetch this session (state workforce agencies, local workforce boards, city/county sites). Verified? = Yes only when the page I opened showed the amount or eligibility written in the row. Rows built from search snippets, older news or pages that blocked me are Verified? = No.
- WebSearch hit its per-turn limit partway through, so Ohio, Pennsylvania, Michigan and Indiana got fewer search-assisted leads than planned. Most local boards publish services but not dollar caps; "not stated on page" is used where the page gave none.
- michigan.gov returned HTTP 403 on every fetch, so every Michigan state-agency page is unread. archive.org is blocked by the tool.
- Counts per state: Kentucky 29 rows (25 verified), Pennsylvania 12 rows (8 verified), Ohio 8 rows (3 verified), Michigan 10 rows (7 verified), Indiana 10 rows (10 verified).
