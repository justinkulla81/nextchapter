# State and local programs, group H (WA, OR, ID, MT, WY, UT, CO, AZ, NM, AK)

Research date: 2026-10-23.

Method and limits: Official pages were fetched with plain HTTP requests this session and read as text. A row is Verified? = Yes only when the amount or eligibility written in the row appears on the official page that was opened. WebSearch hit its session quota mid-run, so discovery was done by following links from agency sites. Arizona agency sites (des.az.gov, azcommerce.com, arizonaatwork.com) return HTTP 403 to automated fetches; for Arizona, des.az.gov pages were read from Internet Archive copies dated 2025-12-08 (marked "via Wayback" in Status) and the Official URL column is the live des.az.gov address. Colorado cwdc.colorado.gov and cdhe.colorado.gov intermittently returned 403 or 404 for deep links; rows built from those hosts say so. WA SBCTC and WSAC pages for amounts are partly script-rendered, so some amounts are not confirmed. Federal-baseline WIOA, TAA and Rapid Response are listed only where the state or local board adds named detail. Employer-only grants (Idaho WDTF, Montana IWT, Wyoming WDTF, Alaska IWTP, Arizona Job Training Program) and institution-only grants (Alaska TVEP) are in the Left out notes, not rows.

Counts (rows / verified Yes): Washington 19 / 15, Oregon 13 / 9, Idaho 12 / 9, Montana 9 / 7, Wyoming 8 / 5, Utah 15 / 13, Colorado 14 / 8, Arizona 13 / 9, New Mexico 11 / 8, Alaska 10 / 8.
