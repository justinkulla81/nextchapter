# State and local programs, sweep A: California, Nevada, Hawaii

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

Rows were built from official pages opened this session with WebFetch. Verified? = Yes only where the amount, eligibility and status written in the row appear on a page I opened (the URL in the row, or the page named in the row text). Many official sites returned 403 or 404 to the fetcher (EDD training pages, ewdd.lacity.gov, ccsf.edu, detr.nv.gov, cccco.edu, csac.ca.gov); those programs are listed with Verified? = No and the basis (search summary of the official page, or news coverage) is stated in Eligibility. The WebSearch quota ran out partway through Nevada, so Nevada and Hawaii got less local depth than planned. Federal baseline WIOA, TAA and SCSEP rules are not repeated except where the state or board adds something. Programs already in data/benefits/state-training.md and state_ai.md (CA ETP homepage, CA Rapid Response, WINN, Hawaii Promise, LearnNV homepage) are not repeated unless re-checked here.

Counts (rows / verified Yes): California 33 / 20, Nevada 12 / 5, Hawaii 13 / 8.
