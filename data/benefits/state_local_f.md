# State and local programs for laid-off and career-changing professionals: IL, WI, MN, IA, MO, KS, NE, ND, SD

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

Each row is something a person can use. Verified? = Yes only where the official page was opened this session and the figures written appear on it. WebFetch returns a model summary of each page, so where a page was thin (many state sites are menu-only) the amount is written as "not stated on page". Rows marked No are from search results or from memory and say so in Eligibility. Federal-baseline WIOA, TAA and RESEA are listed only where the state or a local board adds something specific. Employer-only grants, institution-only grants and expired offers are in the Left out note at the end.

Row counts: Illinois: 15 rows, 10 verified; Wisconsin: 16 rows, 13 verified; Minnesota: 16 rows, 11 verified; Iowa: 16 rows, 14 verified; Missouri: 14 rows, 7 verified; Kansas: 15 rows, 13 verified; Nebraska: 15 rows, 11 verified; North Dakota: 16 rows, 13 verified; South Dakota: 14 rows, 11 verified.
