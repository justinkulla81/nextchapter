# State and local programs, sweep C: NY, NJ, CT, RI, DE

Research date: 2026-10-23

## Method and limits

- Searched with WebSearch, then opened each program's own page with WebFetch. Verified? = Yes only where the page I opened this session showed the eligibility or amount written in the row. Rows marked No came from search snippets or pages that returned 403/404 or unreadable PDFs.
- Many official PDFs (ITA policies for NJ, Capital Region NY, Rochester, RI WorkShare) returned compressed binary that could not be read, so ITA dollar caps are mostly NOT stated. Several state sites (suny.edu, dlt.ri.gov) returned 403.
- Skipped programs already in state-training.md and state_ai.md (e.g. NJ CCOG, DE Workforce Pell, CT WIOA boards) unless re-checked here.
- Counts (rows / verified): New York 18 / 12. New Jersey 10 / 6. Connecticut 8 / 5. Rhode Island 5 / 1. Delaware 5 / 4. The 30+ NY plus NJ target was NOT met (28 rows); Long Island, Albany, Newark and Jersey City have named centers but no verified ITA caps or program details.
