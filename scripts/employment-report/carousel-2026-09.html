3 · What public programs are adding

Workforce Pell opened in July, and Texas is building the outcome data.

Pell aid for short-term training. Programs of 8 to 15 weeks; they must meet 70% completion and 70% job-placement rates. The law starts July 1, 2026; the Education Department's final rule took effect July 20.
Texas approvals. Five community colleges approved for nine programs (health care, welding, machining, electric power), per the governor's October 2 announcement.
$10.4 million Labor Department grant ($10,467,300 over four years) to a 37-college consortium to build the data colleges need to qualify programs.Outcome evidence: none yet.
Sources: U.S. Department of Education (final rule, 91 Fed. Reg. 29254, May 19, 2026); Labor Department; Texas Governor's office (October 2, 2026), per the report (Chapter 13A). One news report counted two approved colleges; the report names both counts.

3 · What public programs are adding

Funding is increasingly tied to outcomes.

Nearly $162M
Registered Apprenticeship pay-for-performance awards, announced July 7, 2026

Sponsors are paid as apprentices reach verified retention and progression milestones, and at least 85% of each award goes to sponsors. Outcome evidence: none yet.

How strong is the evidence? Strong for one program, descriptive for others, and absent for the newest. We say which is which.
Source: Labor Department news release 26-1012-NAT (July 7, 2026), as summarized in the report (Chapter 13A).
