# Personalization spine

Generated by `npm run docs:spine` from `src/lib/personalization/spine.ts`. Do not edit by hand.

Personalization is the foundation for every journey and stage. Each question below is asked because its answer drives something we do: the weekly plan, learning and development, networking, the opportunities we show, the report or the coaching. Nothing is asked that does not drive something, and a test checks that every file named as reading an answer really reads it.

## Every question, what it drives and what it can open

### Who you are

| Question | Stored in | What it drives | Opportunity it can open |
|---|---|---|---|
| What is the highest level you have reached? | `highestLevelReached` | **Your report:** Judges how marketable you are at your level, not against everyone.<br>**Opportunities:** Shows roles at and one step above your level, and hides the rest.<br>**Your weekly plan:** Senior searches get unposted-role steps (outreach and recruiters); early-career gets alumni and first-résumé steps. | A shortlist of roles at your level, and the right kind of company for it. |
| What function do you work in? | `primaryFunction` | **Learning and development:** Picks the courses and certifications for your function.<br>**Networking:** Finds peers in your function in the community and among alumni.<br>**Opportunities:** Sets the market data (pay, demand, who is hiring) we show you. | Courses for your function, peers to talk to, and a market read for your role. |
| How many years of experience do you have? | `yearsExperience` | **Your report:** Calibrates how your experience reads to a hiring manager.<br>**Coaching:** Sets how direct and how senior the coaching is. | A fair read of where you stand, so roles that fit your experience rise to the top. |
