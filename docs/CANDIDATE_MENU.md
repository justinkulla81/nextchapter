# Candidate menu by situation

Generated by `npm run docs:menu` from `src/lib/journey/menu.ts` and `src/lib/nav/places.ts`. Do not edit by hand.

The dashboard has six places (Home, Jobs, Network, Learn, Personalize, Help), each with a tab row on every page.
The member's journey and stage decide which tabs are shown, locked or hidden. The rules are in `src/lib/journey/menu.ts` (tested in `src/test/menu.test.ts` and `src/test/place-tabs-situation.test.ts`).

## Show, lock, or hide

- **Show**: the tool helps in this situation.
- **Locked**: the tool turns on when the member's situation changes. The tab stays, muted, and a "Turn on more tools" box under the tab row says why it is off and updates the status in one click (the same change as "Has anything changed?"). This is how people learn why they should keep their status current.
- **Hidden**: the tool will not matter in this situation or the next one.

Locks for something the member can go and do (connect Gmail, take the Skills Assessment) are separate and keep their own reason. A tab with no situation rule always shows.

There is no "on a break" state for someone in the workforce: someone who is not looking is managing their career, with a plan, a menu and emails of their own. Time off with a return date is its own situation (On leave).

## The situations

| Situation | Who is here |
|---|---|
| Exploring | Thinking about a move or planning ahead; not applying yet. |
| Preparing | Getting ready to apply: résumé, proof, targets. |
| Active | Applying, interviewing or holding an offer. Also members with no stage yet. |
| Managing your career | Growing in a job, not looking right now, or going for a promotion before applying elsewhere. |
| Landed | Started a new job, first 90 days. Moves to Managing your career after 90 days. |
| Portfolio | Happy with fractional work: running a set of clients. |
| On leave | Taking time off with a return date (family leave, sabbatical). |

## Why a tool is off, and how to turn it on

**Exploring.** Job-search tools turn on when you are looking for a new job. Your menu and your weekly plan follow your situation, so you only see what helps now.

- "I'm ready to start applying": Turns on Find a Full-time Job and interview practice, and your plan switches to applications and outreach.
- "I was laid off or let go": Starts a laid-off plan: benefits and severance first, then a fast search. Also tells a college you joined, if you share that.

**Preparing.** Offer and negotiation tools turn on when you are interviewing or have an offer.

- "I'm ready to start applying": Moves you into an active search with weekly application and outreach targets.
- "I'm interviewing": Adds interview practice, reference follow-ups and what to do after each round.

**Managing your career.** Job-search tools turn on when you are looking for a new job. Your menu and your weekly plan follow your situation, so you only see what helps now.

- "I'm ready to start applying": Turns on Find a Full-time Job and interview practice, and your plan switches to applications and outreach.
- "I was laid off or let go": Starts a laid-off plan: benefits and severance first, then a fast search. Also tells a college you joined, if you share that.

**Portfolio.** Full-time job tools turn on when you want a full-time role. Your menu is built around your clients and your pipeline.

- "I'm ready to start applying": Turns on Find a Full-time Job and interview practice, and your plan switches to applications and outreach.
- "I lost my main client or role": Starts a laid-off plan: benefits first, then a fast search.

**On leave.** Job tools are off while you are on a break. Tell us when that changes.

- "I'm back at work": Moves you to managing your career, with a plan for your return.
- "I'm ready to start applying": Turns on Find a Full-time Job and interview practice.
