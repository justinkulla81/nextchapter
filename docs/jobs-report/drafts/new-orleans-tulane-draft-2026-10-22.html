New Orleans, LA: white-collar jobs and employment

Prepared for Tulane University by NextChapter Research · career-site data as of 2026-10-22 · draft

Limited sample. Ochsner health is 47% of all postings (maximum 40%). Treat the shares and pay figures below as describing these employers only, not the local market.

Summary

The sample is 1,423 white-collar postings from 25 employers' own career sites. 73% are health care, 4% are entry-level, 21% are senior individual contributor or above, and median stated pay is $62,390 a year.

  • Labor market: unemployment was 3.9% in 2026-08, against 4.5% a year earlier; nonfarm employment changed 0.3% over twelve months.
  • Entry-level: 52 white-collar postings across 7 employers.
  • AI: 1.5% of white-collar postings mention AI; those pay a median of not stated against $62,390 for the rest.
  • Concentration: Ochsner Health alone is 47% of all postings, so the mix reflects the largest readable employers more than the whole market.

Coverage

1,916 postings in the area were read from 25 employers, and 1,423 of them are white-collar (74%). Another 236 could not be classified from the title and are left out. The frame is every white-collar job at every level, entry-level included (Census major groups 1 and 2).

Employers on career systems we cannot read are missing, so read these figures as describing the employers listed, not every opening in the area.

Large employers we looked for and could not read: Tulane Medical Center, Children's Hospital New Orleans, University Medical Center New Orleans, Touro Infirmary, East Jefferson General Hospital, Xavier University of Louisiana, Loyola University New Orleans, University of New Orleans, Delgado Community College, Dillard University, Southern University at New Orleans, Louisiana State University Health Sciences Center, Entergy, Chevron.

Jobs at risk: AI exposure of the openings

Anthropic rates each occupation's observed exposure to AI, which weighs real usage and automated uses more heavily than hypothetical capability. In this feed 5% of white-collar openings are in highly exposed occupations, 25% in medium and 70% in low. Among entry and early-career openings, 11% are in highly exposed occupations. Exposure is not a forecast of job loss: Anthropic found no systematic rise in unemployment for exposed workers so far, with early signs that hiring of younger workers has slowed in exposed occupations.

Method and limits

  • Source: employers' own career sites, crawled 2026-10-22, tagged by job title with fixed rules; titles that cannot be classified are excluded.
  • AI, remote, hybrid and soft-skill figures are keyword matches in posting text; pay medians use only postings that state pay.
  • Postings are not BLS job openings: one opening can have several postings and some are always open.
  • There is no history yet. Functions gaining or losing, reposts and ghost-job risk need at least two crawls.
  • Labor market data: BLS Local Area Unemployment Statistics and Current Employment Statistics bulk files, not seasonally adjusted.

Sources: employers' own career sites; U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (LAUS, CES); O*NET. Figures describe the employers listed, not every opening in the area.
