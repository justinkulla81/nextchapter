Louisville / Jefferson County, KY: white-collar jobs and employment

Prepared for Louisville and Jefferson County by NextChapter Research · career-site data as of 2026-10-19 · draft

Summary

The sample is 1,782 white-collar postings from 23 employers' own career sites. 61% are health care, 3% are entry-level, 20% are senior individual contributor or above, and median stated pay is $74,202 a year.

  • Labor market: unemployment was 4.4% in 2026-09, against 4.0% a year earlier; nonfarm employment changed -0.0% over twelve months.
  • Entry-level: 55 white-collar postings across 12 employers.
  • AI: 2.9% of white-collar postings mention AI; those pay a median of $126,950 against $73,534 for the rest.
  • Concentration: Norton Healthcare alone is 25% of all postings, so the mix reflects the largest readable employers more than the whole market.

Coverage

2,506 postings in the area were read from 23 employers, and 1,782 of them are white-collar (71%). Another 210 could not be classified from the title and are left out. The frame is every white-collar job at every level, entry-level included (Census major groups 1 and 2).

Employers on career systems we cannot read are missing, so read these figures as describing the employers listed, not every opening in the area.

Large employers we looked for and could not read: Ford Motor Company, Louisville Water Company, Jefferson County Clerk / courts, Veterans Affairs and other federal, LG&E and KU, ScionHealth, BrightSpring Health Services, Deloitte, KPMG, EY, Wells Fargo, Stock Yards Bank and Trust, Jack Henry & Associates, Churchill Downs.

Jobs at risk: AI exposure of the openings

Anthropic rates each occupation's observed exposure to AI, which weighs real usage and automated uses more heavily than hypothetical capability. In this feed 6% of white-collar openings are in highly exposed occupations, 20% in medium and 75% in low. Among entry and early-career openings, 7% are in highly exposed occupations. Exposure is not a forecast of job loss: Anthropic found no systematic rise in unemployment for exposed workers so far, with early signs that hiring of younger workers has slowed in exposed occupations.

Method

Employer lists exclude staffing and search firms, because they post for other employers. Entry-level means roughly 0 to 3 years of experience, read from the title, the employer and the full posting. Employer names are as listed in postings, so a company that posts under several names can appear more than once. Counts are a snapshot of open jobs on 2026-11-16; NextChapter began keeping employer history in October 2026, so this report makes no claim about change from a prior month or year for any employer. Full lists: https://launchyournextchapter.com/top-employers.

Method and limits

  • Source: employers' own career sites, crawled 2026-10-19, tagged by job title with fixed rules; titles that cannot be classified are excluded.
  • AI, remote, hybrid and soft-skill figures are keyword matches in posting text; pay medians use only postings that state pay.
  • Postings are not BLS job openings: one opening can have several postings and some are always open.
  • There is no history yet. Functions gaining or losing, reposts and ghost-job risk need at least two crawls.
  • Labor market data: BLS Local Area Unemployment Statistics and Current Employment Statistics bulk files, not seasonally adjusted.

Sources: employers' own career sites; U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (LAUS, CES); O*NET. Figures describe the employers listed, not every opening in the area.
