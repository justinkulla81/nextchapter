### Method

Employer lists exclude staffing and search firms, because they post for other employers. Entry-level means roughly 0 to 3 years of experience, read from the title, the employer and the full posting. Employer names are as listed in postings, so a company that posts under several names can appear more than once. Counts are a snapshot of open jobs on 2026-10-26; NextChapter began keeping employer history in October 2026, so this report makes no claim about change from a prior month or year for any employer. Full lists: https://launchyournextchapter.com/top-employers.
