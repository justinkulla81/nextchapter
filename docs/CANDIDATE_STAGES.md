# Candidate stages: activities, features, menu and marketing text

Generated by `npm run docs:stages` from `src/lib/journey/stage-guide.ts` and the menu rules in `src/lib/journey/menu.ts`. Do not edit by hand.

A stage is how close someone is to applying. There is no "on a break": someone who is not looking is *managing their career*.
How hard someone is looking (actively or casually) is a separate setting that changes the pace within a stage; see each stage's note.

## Exploring

**What it is for:** Learn your market and your worth. No application targets.

**Who is here:** Curious about the market, or worried about a layoff, and not applying yet. Thinking about a move, Changing careers and Planning the next act start here.

**Marketing text**

> **Know your market before you need it.** See how the market reads you, what you are worth and what would make you ready, privately, with no pressure to apply.
