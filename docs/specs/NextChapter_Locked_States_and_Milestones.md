# NextChapter — Locked States, Milestone Ladder & Coaching Placement

Version 0.1 — what the report shows for work not yet done, and how earned progress unlocks access.

---

## 1. Principles

**Every gate must be real.** Recruiter introductions are gated on Dossier completeness because the Dossier is what gets sent. Candidates detect arbitrary gamification instantly, and it costs more trust than the FOMO is worth.

**Locked slots appear inline**, in the position the earned content would occupy. The report should read as incomplete, not as advertised at.

**The free report stands alone.** Five or six locked slots maximum. Render them lighter than earned content — lower contrast, no color, no badges. A locked section should feel like a room they haven't walked into yet, not a door being held shut.

**Show the shape, not a blank.** A lock that reveals nothing produces frustration. Show the structure, the specific unlock condition, and the time cost.

---

## 2. Three lock types, visually distinct

| Type | Unlocked by | Visual | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Work** | Candidate effort — free | Dotted outline, light gray | Most |
| **Milestone** | Accumulated evidence — free | Solid outline, progress bar | 3 |
| **Paid** | Executive Coach | Distinct treatment, clearly marked | 1 |

Work and Milestone locks must never be visually confused with the paid one. If a candidate thinks free work is a paywall, they stop doing the work.

## 3. Locked slot inventory

### 3.1 What others say about you — *Work*
Sits directly under "What's working."

> **What others say about you** — locked
>
> This is where the strongest section of your report will be: what five people who worked with you independently confirmed about your scope, your follow-through, and how you operate under pressure.
>
> Right now every claim in this report is your own. That's the ceiling on three of your six grades.
>
> **0 of 5 references** · 5 minutes to send the requests · a few days to come back
>
> *Nobody else does this for candidates. It's the reason our Dossier means something.*

### 3.2 How you work — *Work*

> **How you work** — locked
>
> Your resume proves what you did. This proves how you do it — your operating style, where you thrive, what conditions bring out your best. Built on validated instruments, not a personality quiz.
>
> **21 minutes, two assessments.** Do them alone, on your own time.

### 3.3 Who you already know — *Work*

> **Who you already know** — locked
>
> We'll map your network against companies hiring for your target and rank it by how warm each contact actually is — then tell you who to message first, with the message written.
>
> Most people find this list is far bigger than they expected.
>
> **One click.** Connect LinkedIn and Gmail.

### 3.4 What you're drawn to — *Work*
Replaces the current "Not enough signal yet" empty state.

> **What you're drawn to** — locked
>
> Once you've reviewed a handful of roles, we'll show you the pattern in what you're actually gravitating toward. It's often not what people say they want.
>
> **0 of 5 jobs reviewed.**

### 3.5 How your search is converting — *Work*

> **How your search is converting** — locked
>
> Applications sent, responses back, interviews reached — and where in the funnel you're losing people. This is how you find out whether the problem is your resume, your targeting, or your volume.
>
> **Connect Gmail** and we'll track it automatically, or log applications manually.

### 3.6 How you compare — *Work*

> **How you compare** — locked
>
> Where you stand against other candidates targeting similar roles in your market — on evidence, on corroboration, on search activity.
>
> **Complete your profile** to unlock. About 3 minutes.

### 3.7 Your trajectory — *Work*

> **Your trajectory** — locked
>
> Your grade over time. Most candidates who do the work move a full band in the first three weeks — this is where you'll watch it happen.
>
> **Unlocks after your second week.**

## 4. The milestone ladder

Three rungs. Each gate reflects genuine readiness, and the copy says why.

### Rung 1 — Dossier Preview
**Requires:** 2 references + 1 assessment

> **Your Executive Dossier** — 2 of 8 pieces in place
>
> The Dossier is the document that goes to recruiters and hiring managers: your record, what your references independently confirmed, and how you operate. It's the thing candidates walk into interviews with that nobody else has.
>
> See a preview at two references.

### Rung 2 — Recruiter introductions
**Requires:** Dossier complete — 5 references, both assessments, profile complete

> **Introductions to our executive recruiter network** — locked
>
> We work with search firms placing at your level. When we introduce you, we send your Dossier.
>
> **That's why this is gated.** A recruiter who receives a profile with no corroboration doesn't take the next one seriously — so we don't send them. Complete your Dossier and we make the introduction.
>
> **5 of 8 pieces remaining.**

### Rung 3 — Unposted roles
**Requires:** Dossier complete + 4 consecutive Weekly Search Sprints

> **Roles that never get posted** — locked
>
> Most roles at your level are filled before a posting exists. We see a portion of them through our recruiter and portfolio relationships.
>
> These go to candidates who are credible on paper and actively searching — because when we forward someone, they need to respond that week. Complete your Dossier and hold four consecutive Sprint weeks.
>
> **Dossier: 2 of 8** · **Sprint streak: 0 of 4**

## 5. Coaching placement

Contextual, not universal. A pitch on every report reads as an ad; a pitch at the right moment reads as an answer.

### 5.1 Triggers

| Situation | Copy angle |
|---|---|
| Interviews but no offers | The resume is working. Something in the room isn't. This is what mock interviews are for. |
| D or F band | Self-serve fixes get you partway. This is a rebuild, and it's faster with someone in it with you. |
| Selected interview nerves, self-doubt, or "don't know what I want" | Named the hesitation — coaching is the direct answer to this one. |
| Executive band | At your level the search is positioning and relationships. A coach who's placed at this level is worth more than any tool. |
| Two consecutive weeks with no Sprint completion | Momentum problem, not an information problem. |

### 5.2 Placement

One instance per report, inside the relevant section — not in the header, not repeated. Where an interview-conversion problem is identified, the coaching pitch belongs in that section, not appended at the end.

### 5.3 Copy

> **Want a real person in this with you?**
>
> Everything above stays free. Executive Coach adds a human who's placed people at your level — mock interviews, positioning, a second read on hard calls, and someone who notices when you stall.
>
> Most of what's in your way right now is the kind of thing that's faster to solve out loud.

## 6. Guardrails

**Never lock something the candidate already earned.** If they have three references, show the three-reference version — don't withhold until five.

**Never lock the diagnosis.** What's wrong and how to fix it is always free. Locks apply to *added* evidence and *access*, never to the honest assessment.

**Locked copy is factual, never taunting.** No "you're missing out," no urgency timers, no artificial scarcity.

**Count locks per report and cap it.** More than six and the report stops being a report.

**Progress must be visible immediately.** When a reference comes back, the counter moves and the section partially fills that day. Delayed feedback kills the loop.

**Re-check the tone at the low band.** A D-band candidate has more locks than an A-band one — the people with the least evidence see the most gates. Verify that report reads as a path rather than a wall.
