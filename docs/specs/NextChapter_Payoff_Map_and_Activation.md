## 2. Re-entering: score it honestly, then own the fix

The gap gets scored. Employers penalize it, sometimes unfairly, and a report that hides that is useless on the one point this candidate most needs help with.

What changes is what comes next.

> **Your gap will cost you screenings. We're not going to pretend otherwise.**
>
> Three years out of the workforce reads as risk to a hiring manager who has forty resumes and four minutes. That's not a judgment on you — it's what happens when someone has no recent evidence to look at.
>
> **Here's the thing: recent evidence is buildable, and it's the whole reason this platform exists.**
>
> A three-month interim engagement gives you a current role on your resume, three quantified results, and two people who can vouch for work you did this year. That combination doesn't reduce the gap — it ends it.
>
> We don't place you. What we do is show you where the fractional and interim boards actually are, pass along board and advisory openings we hear about, and walk you through setting yourself up as an independent consultant. Then we turn those engagements into verified references in your Dossier.
>
> Most people returning to work try to explain the gap. The ones who get hired replace it.

That's the pattern for every weakness: name it honestly, then show the specific mechanism that closes it.

## 3. Weakness → payoff map

### 3.1 No references / no third-party evidence
**The unique one. Lead with it.**

> Right now every claim on your resume is your word. A hiring manager discounts that — not because they think you're lying, but because everyone's resume says good things.
>
> **This is the single biggest thing we do that nothing else does.** We run structured reference checks — five to seven people, rotating question sets so answers aren't rehearsed, scored consistently. The result goes into your Executive Dossier: a document that says what other people independently confirmed about your work.
>
> Candidates walk into interviews with corroboration already in hand. Nobody else gives you that.

**Score impact:** three character dimensions are capped until references land. 0→5 is the largest single move available.

### 3.2 Employment gap
> A gap gets you screened out before anyone reads the rest. We close it two ways: access to the fractional, interim, and board openings that put current work back on your resume, and Dossier context that gives a reviewer the answer before they have to ask. We don't place you into work — we show you where it is and how to go get it.

### 3.3 ATS filtering
> Most resumes die here, and most tools give you one generic "ATS score" that means nothing.
>
> **We test against the actual parsing engines** — the ones Workday, Taleo, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby and the rest run on — and show you a matrix of exactly which systems lose which parts of your resume. Then we fix them.
>
> That's the difference between "your resume might have issues" and "Workday recovers 6 of your 9 job titles, here's why."

### 3.4 No stated target
> Without a target, nothing can be matched to you and no reviewer knows what you're arguing for. We build it with you from your own history — then everything downstream sharpens: job matching, your Search Action Plan, your outreach scripts, your Marketing Plan.

### 3.5 No networking activity
> Most roles at your level are filled before they're posted. We turn "network more" into something you can actually do: we build your list from your own history, rank it by warmth, hand you the script, and tell you who to message this week.
>
> Plus direct access to our recruiter network and roles that never hit a job board.

### 3.6 Unproven character traits
> Resumes prove what you did. They can't prove how you work.
>
> Our assessments — the Executive Operating Profile and Executive Personality Profile — are built on validated public-domain instruments, not a personality quiz. They produce a real read on how you operate and where you'll thrive, and it goes into your Dossier alongside your references.

### 3.7 Skills gap
> We identify the *specific* missing skill for your target — not generic upskilling — and route you to the shortest credible path to it, then help you translate it into a resume line and a LinkedIn post.

### 3.8 Job hopping / short tenures
> A reviewer sees three short stints and assumes the worst available explanation. Your Dossier gets ahead of it: your context in one line, plus references who confirm the work was good.

### 3.9 No market presence
> Your Marketing Plan turns visibility into a weekly routine — what to post, when, with direct LinkedIn posting built in. Recruiters find people who are visible.

### 3.10 Interview problems
> If you're getting interviews and not offers, the resume isn't the problem. Executive Coach adds a human — mock interviews, positioning, a second opinion on hard calls.

### 3.11 Not enough relevant roles
> We crawl hundreds of company career pages directly, filtered to director-and-above, matched to your stated target. Not a job board aggregator — the actual source.

### 3.12 Small stuff — no pitch
Typos, date formats, spacing, "References available upon request." Just the fix. Routing these to product cheapens the claims above.

## 4. Activation sequence

### 4.1 Governing principle

**Do the fast things first. Start the slow things early.** Fast feedback proves the loop works; long-latency items need the clock running from session one.

**Every gate opens from the lock screen itself.** If unlocking requires navigating to settings, the gate becomes a wall. The unlock action is always one tap away, inline.

### 4.2 Sequence

| # | Action | Effort | Why here |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Resume fixes** | 20 min | Fully in their control, fastest visible feedback. Proves effort moves the grade. |
| 2 | **Connect LinkedIn + Gmail** | 1 click | The magic moment. Opens four modules at once. |
| 3 | **Send reference requests** | 5 min | Longest latency in the product. Start the clock. |
| 4 | **Two gating question modules** | 5 min | Open pages they'll hit in week one. |
| 5 | **Assessments and skills** | Self-paced | The 2am work. |

### 4.3 Connect both together

LinkedIn and Gmail produce something neither does alone. LinkedIn shows who someone knows; Gmail shows who they're actually in touch with. The intersection is warmth ranking — the thing that makes an outreach list usable instead of theoretical.

> **Connect LinkedIn and Gmail.**
>
> LinkedIn shows us who you know. Gmail shows us who you're actually in touch with. Together we can tell you who's genuinely warm — and who to message first.
>
> *We read only what's relevant to your search: who you've corresponded with and when, plus application confirmations and recruiter replies. We never send anything, and we never touch unrelated mail.*
>
> **[Connect both]** · *Connect LinkedIn only*

Keep the LinkedIn-only fallback visible but secondary — Gmail is the scarier ask and shouldn't take LinkedIn down with it. Anything gated on Gmail alone must degrade gracefully rather than staying fully locked.

**Payoff copy:**

> **23 people you know at companies hiring for your target.** You've emailed 6 of them in the past year — start there.

Gmail stays behind CASA. Design it now, ship it when compliance clears.

### 4.4 Question modules

One container, chunked. Each module shows its time cost and what it opens. Progress saves; stopping mid-module costs nothing.

| Module | Time | Opens |
|---|---|---|
| **How you'll search** — networking comfort, the real hesitation, outreach target, public visibility | 2 min | My Network, outreach scripts, Marketing Plan |
| **What's in your way** — blockers, motivations, coaching tone, pace *(private to coach)* | 3 min | Coach strategy review |
| **Your preferences** — comp floor, industries, company size, direction, work authorization | 3 min | Matched jobs, peer comparison |
| **How I Work Best** — 56 items | 12 min | Operating Profile |
| **How I Perform** — 40 items | 9 min | Personality Profile |
| **Skills** | 10 min | Learning, webinars, interim work |

The first two go into activation because they open pages candidates hit in week one. A candidate who clicks My Network on day one and finds a locked form has been punished for engaging.

**Ask at the door, too.** Anyone who skips activation and navigates straight to a gated page gets the relevant 2-minute module inline — same questions, no dead end.

### 4.5 Activation screen

> **You've got the diagnosis. Here's what moves it.**
>
> **1. Fix three things on your resume** — 20 min. Re-upload and watch the grade move.
> **2. Connect LinkedIn and Gmail** — one click. See who you already know at companies hiring.
> **3. Send five reference requests** — 5 min. These take days to come back, so start now.
> **4. Two quick question sets** — 5 min. Opens My Network and your coach's review.

Everything else lives on the dashboard with a progress bar, available whenever.

### 5.1 On jobs

**Jobs are the engine, not the exception.** Candidates arrive wanting roles, and that desire is the strongest lever the product will ever have. Spending it on an unlocked browse is a waste.

Show the count and the titles — proof the roles are real. Gate detail and apply behind one reference request.

The gate is honest because it *is* the diagnosis:

> **You've sent over 100 applications and had no interviews.**
>
> We're not going to help you send the 101st. That channel isn't returning anything for you, and more volume won't change it.
>
> **47 roles match your target.** They open when you send your reference requests — five minutes, and they take a few days to come back. An application with corroboration behind it is a different application.

For a fresh candidate with no application history:

> **47 roles match your target.**
>
> They'll be here. First, send your reference requests — five minutes now, a few days to come back. Every week you wait is a week your Dossier is empty when someone asks for it.
>
> You will never have more energy for this than you do today.

**The principle:** front-load the hard, slow, high-value things while motivation is at its peak. Energy decays from signup. A candidate who browses on day one and leaves never comes back to do references.

- **10–12 min** → Learning, webinars, interim work
- **~8 min** → Community
- **Days** → Dossier preview

## 6. Partial connection: degrade visibly, not silently

A LinkedIn-only candidate gets a working module with the Gmail-dependent features shown as locked. Not hidden, not silently absent — visibly missing, with the shape of what they'd get.

**My Network — LinkedIn connected, Gmail not:**

> **847 connections mapped. 23 at companies hiring for your target.**
>
> **Who to message first** — locked
> We can see who you know. We can't see who you're actually in touch with, so we can't tell you who's warm. Connect Gmail and this list reorders by who'll actually reply.
>
> **Your last contact with each person** — locked
> **Draft messages that reference your real history** — locked

**My Contacts — fully gated on Gmail**, showing the shape:

> **Your contacts** — locked
> Everyone you've corresponded with in the last two years, filtered to your industry and level, ranked by recency. Most candidates find 40 to 60 people here they'd forgotten they knew.

This is deliberate. Locked features next to working ones are the clearest possible statement of what the connection buys.

Any badge. Whichever hard thing they do first opens the room.

> **The NextChapter community** — locked

This rewards doing *a* hard thing rather than one specific thing, and it means the room is populated by people with momentum rather than people who found the sign-up button.

## 8. Community moderation

Job-seeker communities are a known target for fraud and a genuine duty-of-care surface. AI moderation runs on every post, with human escalation.

| Category | Action |
|---|---|
| **Recruitment fraud** — advance-fee scams, fake recruiters, resume-rewrite fees, MLM pitches, "unlimited income" posts | Auto-hold, human review before publish. Highest priority — job seekers are a prime target and members are financially stressed. |
| **Crisis and self-harm signals** | **Never auto-delete.** Route to human review immediately, surface Support During Transition to the poster. Deleting a distress post is the worst available outcome. |
| **Harassment, abuse, discriminatory language** | Auto-hold, human review |
| **Doxxing and PII** — resumes with contact details, screenshots naming individuals | Auto-remove, notify poster |
| **Defamation** — specific accusations against named employers or individuals | Auto-hold. Employer criticism is legitimate; unverified accusations against named people are not. |
| **Spam and off-topic promotion** | Auto-remove |
| **Bad legal or financial advice** — severance, unemployment, non-competes, discrimination claims | Flag with a standing note pointing to qualified professionals |

### 8.1 Design choices that reduce moderation load

- **Prompted formats over an open feed** — introductions, wins, specific asks. Open feeds drift toward commiseration.
- **Segment by seniority band**, function second. The advice doesn't transfer across levels and the frustration compounds when it's mixed.
- **No DMs at launch.** Direct messaging is where fraud moves once it's blocked in public.
- **Human moderator from day one.** This is not a set-and-forget surface.

## 9. Guardrails

**Never gate the diagnosis.** What's wrong and how to fix it is always free. Gates apply to added evidence, tooling, and access.

**Gates must be honest and stated.** Every lock says why. "Applications alone aren't working for you" is a reason. "Upgrade to continue" is not.

**Every gate opens inline**, from the lock screen, in five minutes or less for everything in week one.

**Degrade visibly.** Partial connection shows what's missing rather than silently omitting it.

**Check the low band.** Candidates with the least evidence see the most gates. Verify the D-band experience reads as a route forward rather than a wall.

**Never promise placement.** Interim, fractional, and board access means surfacing openings and teaching the playbook. We don't place people, and no copy anywhere should imply we do.

**The movement has to be real.** Take the D-band fixture, apply exactly the three fixes its report recommends, re-score. It must move at least a full band. Verify against the live scorer rather than assuming.
