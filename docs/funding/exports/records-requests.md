# Public-records requests: who holds the workforce technology and training contracts in each state

Drafts for review. Nothing here has been sent. Each state has its own rules on who may request, how (email, portal or mail), fees and response time; check the agency's records page before sending, and send from a company address you can receive replies at. The statute names are the common names of each state's law; confirm the current citation if a lawyer will sign.

## The letter (insert the state, agency and law from each section)

> Dear Records Officer,
>
> Under the [LAW], I request copies of the following records held by [AGENCY]:
>
> 1. Every contract, amendment, renewal and sole-source justification since January 1, 2019 with any of these vendors: Geographic Solutions, FutureFit AI, Lightcast (Emsi Burning Glass), WIN Learning, Eightfold AI, Coursera, Skillsoft, Salesforce (for workforce case management), Social Solutions, CaseWorthy, CareerTeam, myOneFlow, Monster Government Solutions, Jobs for the Future.
> 2. For each, the solicitation (RFP, RFQ or other), all bids or proposals received, the scoring sheets, and the award notice.
> 3. For any contract in item 1 with a term ending before December 31, 2028, the expiration date, option years and the planned re-procurement date.
> 4. Any current or planned solicitation for a labor exchange or job bank, WIOA case management, career navigation, AI career coaching, labor market information, rapid-response or layoff-support platform or service.
> 5. The agency's current vendor list or registration instructions for these services, and its small-purchase and sole-source thresholds.
>
> I ask for electronic copies. Please tell me in advance if fees will exceed $50. If any part is withheld, please cite the exemption and release the rest.
>
> Thank you,
> [Name], [Title], NextChapter, [phone], [email]

## Where to start

The states with the most known incumbent activity: Texas, California, Ohio, Michigan, North Carolina (high layoffs and a Reskill award), then New Mexico, New Hampshire, Tennessee, Oregon, Iowa and the Virgin Islands (Geographic Solutions contracts already seen, with end dates to confirm). Kentucky is already known through the South Central board.
