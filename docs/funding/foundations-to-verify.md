# Foundations to verify

Candidate foundations that fund workforce transitions and mid-career education.
Checked 2026-10-13 by web search and by reading each funder's own page where it
would load. **Nobody has been contacted and no form was filled.** Twin of the
typed list in `src/lib/funding-opportunities/foundations.ts`; keep them in step.

NextChapter is a for-profit company. Most of these funders limit grants to
501(c)(3) public charities, so the for-profit column is the first thing to read.

- **VERIFIED**: program name, access route and eligibility wording were read on the funder's own page on the date checked. "Not stated" is a valid finding when the page names who can apply but not for-profits.
- **TO VERIFY**: only secondary sources, the funder's page would not load, or its own pages do not say who can apply. Treat nothing in these rows as fact.

Access path key: **Direct** (NextChapter as grantee), **College or board** (inside a college or workforce-board grantee), **Nonprofit partner** (inside a nonprofit or intermediary grantee), **Not accessible**. On TO VERIFY rows the path is provisional.

## Detail

### Lumina Foundation: VERIFIED (2026-10-13)
- Program: proactive grantmaking on postsecondary attainment; occasional open RFPs.
- Funds: postsecondary access and attainment, mostly with chosen partners.
- Who can apply: its grants page says grantmaking is primarily proactive and it "does not accept or respond to unsolicited requests/letters of inquiry". It occasionally issues open RFPs on specific topics. The pages read do not state eligible organization types. Older third-party listings say 501(c)(3) public charities (secondary, unconfirmed).
- For-profit: does not state.
- Access path: college or board. Watch for an RFP or be named by a partner it funds.
- Source: https://www.luminafoundation.org/grants/

### Strada Education Foundation: VERIFIED (2026-10-13)
- Programs: Beyond Completion Challenge, Intermediary Learning Collaborative grants, Innovation in Career Services grants (Employer and Community College Partnership Challenge ran 2022 to 2025).
- Funds: education-to-employment pathways, career services, employer and college partnerships.
- Who can apply: "We provide grants to nonprofit organizations, postsecondary education providers, and workforce and skills-based educators." Whether a for-profit counts as a skills-based educator, and whether applications are open or invited, is not stated on the page read.
- For-profit: does not state.
- Access path: college or board. Ask through a college partner first.
- Source: https://www.strada.org/how-we-work/grantmaking

### Walmart.org: VERIFIED (2026-10-13). EXCLUDES FOR-PROFITS
- Programs: Creating Opportunity priority area (invitation-only); Spark Good Local Community Grants.
- Who can apply: U.S. funding is generally limited to IRS-recognized public charities and equivalents. Most focus areas are invitation only: "Unsolicited proposals will not be accepted, and invitations are not granted upon request." Local Community Grants is the only program that takes applications all year.
- Access path: nonprofit partner. Only as a vendor inside a nonprofit or college grantee.
- Source: https://walmart.org/how-we-give/grant-eligibility

### Siegel Family Endowment: VERIFIED (2026-10-13). EXCLUDES FOR-PROFITS
- Program: Learning, Work and Infrastructure areas (future of work and AI).
- Who can apply: "Siegel only accepts proposals by invitation." Grants go to or through U.S. section 501(c)(3) public charities. Occasional open calls.
- Access path: nonprofit partner. A 501(c)(3) partner would hold the grant.
- Source: https://www.siegelendowment.org/what-we-fund/

### OpenAI Foundation: VERIFIED (2026-10-13). NO CALL OPEN
- Program: Economic Futures, an initial $250 million for grants, partnerships and direct work, announced May 27, 2026. Three areas: understanding the shift (measurement and forecasting), supporting the transition (job search support, help translating experience into new roles, pilots), building long-term economic security. The "supporting the transition" text names retraining as only part of the answer and says it will fund "pilots at a meaningful scale."
- Who can apply: the announcement says the $250M "will support external organizations through grants, open calls, and institutional partnerships." It does not say whether for-profits qualify and gives no application process. First initiatives were to be announced later in 2026; none was on the foundation's news page on 2026-10-13. The earlier seed quote ("non-profits as well as a wide range of other organizations") is from a news report and is not on the foundation's own page.
- Separate fund: the People-First AI Fund ($50 million, 163 organizations in 2026, closed July 15, 2026) required "U.S.-Based 501(c)(3) Organization" status and a $500,000 to $10 million budget. It excludes for-profits and is aimed at community nonprofits, not workforce transitions.
- For-profit: does not state (Economic Futures); excludes for-profits (People-First AI Fund).
- Access path: direct, if an Economic Futures open call or pilot opens. The foundation runs an email list for grant opportunities; nothing was signed up for.
- Note: our voice is that AI is not shown to cause the long-search rise. Decide how an AI-lab-linked funder fits before approaching.
- Sources: https://openaifoundation.org/news/economic-futures-in-the-age-of-ai and https://openaifoundation.org/news/2026-people-first-ai-fund

### Ascendium Education Group: VERIFIED (2026-10-13)
- Programs: three strategies, Expand Opportunity, Support Learner Success, and Connect and Align Systems, with exploration, validation and scaling grants. Workforce training sits inside these strategies.
- Funds: postsecondary education and workforce training outcomes for learners from low-income backgrounds (its FAQ names adult learners), usually through intermediaries or networks of institutions, not single colleges.
- Who can apply: grant partners include "postsecondary education and workforce training providers, intermediaries, researchers, and media organizations"; most opportunities are "by invitation only". The grant portal page says it does not accept unsolicited letters of inquiry or proposals. Its FAQ says RFPs are occasional and limited, and it welcomes a brief outreach email to philanthropy@ascendiumeducation.org. No page read states whether for-profits qualify. No grant size stated.
- For-profit: does not state.
- Access path: nonprofit partner (intermediary, college or training provider). Outreach has not been sent.
- Fit: low-income learner focus differs from the mid-career professional market.
- Source: https://www.ascendiumphilanthropy.org/our-approach/grantmaking (the older ascendiumeducation.org address returns 403).

### Gates Foundation: VERIFIED (2026-10-13)
- Program: Postsecondary Success (Innovation, Transformation, Data and information, Policy). 2026 AI Transformation Learning Grants, closed September 11, 2026, about $150,000 to $200,000 each for up to eight institutions.
- Who can apply: its funding page says "We generally do not accept unsolicited proposals" and that it identifies organizations to carry out projects it starts. The same page says its charitable funding to organizations, "including private sector organizations", comes with public-access conditions. The grant opportunities page says most grants go to 501(c)(3) and other tax-exempt organizations identified by staff. The AI call was for "broad-access colleges and universities."
- For-profit: allows for-profits in principle, with conditions.
- Access path: college or board. Gates also funds the Innovative Postsecondary Models Fund below.
- Source: https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/our-funding (also https://www.gatesfoundation.org/about/how-we-work/grant-opportunities and https://usprogram.gatesfoundation.org/news-and-insights/newsletter/postsecondary-success-notes-august-2026)

### Ballmer Group: VERIFIED (2026-10-13)
- Program: economic mobility grantmaking by impact area. The public grantee list tags grants "Career Success" and "Tech & Data" (for example aiEDU, $1,000,000, 2026 to 2027).
- Who can apply: "we cannot accept unsolicited grant applications." The pages read do not state eligible organization types or mention for-profits. Listed grants are multi-year; examples run from $750,000 to $20,200,000.
- For-profit: does not state.
- Access path: not accessible directly. Focus is children and families.
- Source: https://ballmergroup.org/our-grants/

### Innovative Postsecondary Models Fund (Panorama Global): VERIFIED (2026-10-13). ALLOWS FOR-PROFITS. CLOSED
- Program: pooled fund from Ascendium, College Futures Foundation, ECMC Foundation, Gates, Haas Jr. Fund, Lumina, Raikes and Strada. Four themes, including Career and Workforce Alignment.
- Grants: 7 to 10 grants of $750,000 to $1,000,000, February 2027 to July 2028.
- Who can apply: open RFP. FAQ: a nonprofit is not required; it will consider "nonprofit organizations, for-profit organizations, and international organizations." Fiscally sponsored applicants and unaccredited providers can apply. Requirements: the model awards degrees or credentials of value, serves U.S. learners, and has enrolled learners by March 31, 2027. Post-baccalaureate certificate models are not eligible.
- Status: "closed to new applications" (deadline September 14, 2026). Applicants hear by November 30, 2026; awards January 2027.
- Access path: direct. Fit is doubtful because NextChapter does not award credentials. Watch for a second round.
- Source: https://panoramaglobal.org/request-for-proposals-innovative-postsecondary-models-fund/

## What this means

- Of the nine VERIFIED rows, two allow for-profits (Gates, in principle and by invitation only; the Innovative Postsecondary Models Fund, now closed), two exclude them by their own wording (Walmart.org and Siegel), and five do not say. Every route found is either closed or invitation only.
- The OpenAI Foundation Economic Futures program is the one live-looking direct route: its stated work covers career transitions and pilots, but no call has opened and eligibility is not stated. Watch its news page.
- Four funders (Ascendium, Gates, Lumina, Strada) sit in the Innovative Postsecondary Models Fund. A college or nonprofit grantee of any of them is the realistic path.
- The most practical first step is not to ask any of them for a grant. It is to list the colleges, boards and nonprofits NextChapter works with that already hold these funders' grants, and ask whether a NextChapter provider line fits their next proposal.
- Three rows stay TO VERIFY (Markle, Google.org, JPMorganChase). Do not approach them until read on the funders' own pages.
