# Network as circles

Status: design and one preview screen (sample data). Not built.

## The idea

Your network is five circles, from the people closest to you to the wider market. One page shows all
five at once, each with where it stands and one next step. Each circle also has its own page for depth.
A separate "This week" page is where you act, drawing people from every circle.

| Circle | Who | What is there today |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Your close circle | Partner, family, friends, and your coach (the empty coach seat is shown) | Supporters, coaching |
| 2. People you know | Contacts, references, people from Gmail and calendar | Contacts, references, follow-ups |
| 3. Alumni and groups | Schools, past employers, outplacement and program groups | Groups, universities, past jobs |
| 4. Industry and function | Communities by field, such as finance and operations | Community |
| 5. Recruiters | Recruiters who can find you (Candidate+) | Recruiter requests |

## Together or separate

- **Overview together.** One screen, five circles, one next step each. This is the map.
- **Circles separate.** Their rules differ: close is personal and you control what is shared, recruiters
  need Candidate+, communities are public. Each circle keeps its own page, with small switches across the
  top to move between them.
- **Action together.** You think "who should I talk to this week", not "which network". The plan draws
  five people from across the circles, with what to ask each: a warm intro, a short chat, an offer to
  help, a reply to a recruiter.

## Menu

Network shows three entries instead of nine: Your network (the circles), This week, Messages.

## Awareness, not pressure

An empty seat is shown plainly: "No coach yet. A coach reviews your plan and keeps you accountable week to
week." Recruiters show how close you are to Candidate+. Nothing implies a statistic we do not have.

## Open decisions

1. Is "People you know" its own circle? I made it one, because contacts are your biggest asset.
2. Order of the rings. Mine is closest to widest: close, people you know, alumni and groups, industry and
   function, recruiters.
3. Employed members: show Recruiters at all? Confidential mode: hide circle 1 and 5.
